Review revision C of the Prysm LED hardware read-only using actual CAD and
harness topology, not only passing ERC/DRC reports. Read README.txt, bom.csv,
build.py, verify.py, all THREE KiCad projects and their fabrication exports.

This is a personal hobby prototype. The Pi5 is separately USB-C powered.
Strip: BTF SK9822 variant45759013650658,5m60LED/m,12mm black PCB IP30;
about168 LEDs installed. Supply: Mean Well LPV-100-5,5V12A60W. User will cap
final linear LED output at75%; this is not a hardware current/fault limit.

Two BTF T4pin12mm corners at top-right/bottom-left are intentionally retained.
Their mapping/current/fit remain unverified commissioning blockers. Each third
arm receives ONLY+5/GND using a removable passive power tongue and XT30 lead.
No DATA/CLOCK wires or active LED on that arm; verify DO->DI and CO->CI through
the adjacent arms. Do not infer digital pad order from analog RGB markings.

Signal chain: GPIO->TOP->top-right T->RIGHT->active bottom-right corner PCB
->BOTTOM->bottom-left T->LEFT. TOP and RIGHT are fused A; BOTTOM and LEFT are
fused B. Top-left start/end gap stays open. User approved the active corner
instead of the old passive L. No passive power/ground/signal bypass there.

GPIO and corner use ISO7720FD (D8 SOIC,default LOW,two forward channels),
not AHCT125. Pi side powers from header3.3V; GPIO output side from local TOP
power/GND. Corner input side powers from RIGHT(A); output side from BOTTOM(B).
No separate logic cable from distribution. Test actual absence of parallel
LED return paths through Pi/USB/HDMI, signal wires and the opposite strip.
Independently check partial supply loss and missing branch returns, local
input rails, decoupling, output waveform and the actual KiCad D8 pinout.

Check 2oz distribution copper,1oz logic planes/stubs/vias, fuse versus LPV
hiccup behavior, current and temperature at every clip/tongue/connector,
removability, insulation/strain relief, GPIO underside orientation and Pi
cooler fit. Verify BOM mating parts and wire/contact ratings with manufacturer
sources. Verify generator reproducibility and fresh export/ZIP consistency.
Separate CAD-confirmed corrections from unmeasured physical risks. Conclude
whether bare-board prototyping and behind-TV operation are ready separately.
